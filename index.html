Welcome to BAABFest

Why You Should Join Us at BAABFest

We are a consortium of like-minded creatives within Edo state who share a vision of achieving connectivity, visibility, accessibility, and enlightenment (CAVE) amongst budding and seasoned writers and artists in and from Edo state.

  • A platform of visibility for established and budding writers and art creators
  • An all-inclusive space for creative ideas that chronicle our time
  • Connecting Nigerian artists in the diaspora with their roots

The Rongo Residency

A Sanctuary for Artists

Inspired by the Māori concept of Rongo — peace and harmony — this residency is a retreat for deep reflection and artistic growth. Connect with expert mentors while focusing entirely on your craft in a serene environment.

Apply for the Residency
01
Fully Funded

Accommodation in a peaceful, inspiring setting plus transportation stipends for residents outside Benin City.

02
Daily Meals & Workspace

Daily meals to fuel creativity without logistical burdens, plus equipment and workspace tailored to your discipline.

03
Expert Mentorship

Mentorship from leading professionals in your field to help you grow and refine your practice.

04
Structured Schedule

A structured residency schedule provided upon acceptance, designed to maximize productivity.
