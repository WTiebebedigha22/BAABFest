Frequently Asked Questions

Is attendance free?

Yes! BAABFest is committed to accessibility. Festival events, panels and open mics are open to all who register.

Who can apply for the residency?

Artists who are current residents of Nigeria with at least 3 years of active practice in their discipline.

Can I volunteer?

Absolutely! We welcome volunteers who want to make a difference. Sign up via the volunteer registration link.

BAABFest

The Benin Arts and Books Festival is a platform of visibility and connection between established and budding writers and all forms of art creators in Edo state and those in the diaspora.
