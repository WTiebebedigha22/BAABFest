About the Residency

Peace, Harmony & Creative Growth

Inspired by the Māori concept of Rongo — peace and harmony — this residency is a sanctuary for artists to create away from the bustle of everyday life. Designed as a retreat for deep reflection and artistic growth, the program connects residents with expert mentors while providing a serene environment to focus entirely on their craft.

Each cycle rotates between disciplines, ensuring a curated experience for all participants.

Benefits

What Residents Receive

Fully Funded Support

  • Accommodation
    Fully funded accommodation in a peaceful, inspiring setting.
  • Daily Meals
    Daily meals to fuel creativity without logistical burdens.
  • Transportation Stipends
    Transportation stipends for residents outside Benin City.

Creative Development

  • Equipment & Workspace
    Access to equipment and workspace tailored to your discipline.
  • Expert Mentorship
    Mentorship from leading professionals in your field.
  • Structured Schedule
    A structured residency schedule (provided upon acceptance) to maximize productivity.
Who Can Apply?

Eligibility & Disciplines

Artistic Disciplines
  • Writers — fiction, nonfiction, poetry
  • Visual Artists — painters, sculptors, filmmakers, photographers
  • Theatre & Performing Artists — actors, directors, performance poets, choreographers
Eligibility Requirements
  • Must be a current resident of Nigeria
  • Must have at least 3 years of active practice in their artistic discipline
